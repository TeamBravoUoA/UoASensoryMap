      Space types

      Inside each place, individual spaces are tagged with one of these types. Use the chips on the map to filter places by the space types they offer.

      Help

      Browse the map to find places on campus. Click a marker to see details, opening hours, facilities and sensory profiles.

      Filter places with the chips at the top or the sidebar panel. You can filter by campus, category, space type, quiet zone and neurodivergent-safe spaces.

      Space types describe the kinds of spaces inside each building, such as study rooms, quiet areas, social lounges, food & drink, facilities and sensory rooms.

      Sensory ring colour around each marker shows the average sensory intensity: green is low/calm, yellow is moderate, red is high.

      Places page lists all locations in a card view. Open it from the top navigation or the floating Places button.

      Feedback can be left from any location's detail panel to help keep the map accurate.
